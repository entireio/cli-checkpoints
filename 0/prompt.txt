firstSymlinkedEntry only checks entry.Type()&fs.ModeSymlink. On filesystems where os.ReadDir can’t populate the type bits (i.e., entry.Type()==0), this will miss symlinks entirely and allow a symlinked .entire/<entry> to pass validation.

Consider falling back to entry.Info() when entry.Type()==0, skipping entries that vanished (fs.ErrNotExist) and treating other Info() errors as ErrEntireDirUnreadable.
