One residual edge, newly introduced by the read-chain fix

Toby derived the displaced remote from current config instead of persisting it, reasoning it "answers the same question while the remote exists." It answers it as of now, and the answer moves when config does — LegacyCheckpointRemote prefers origin unconditionally:

step 1: election="gh" (sole)   <- checkpoints accumulate here
step 2: add Entire remote  readChain=[ent gh]
step 3: add an origin      readChain=[ent origin]
RESULT: "gh" (which actually holds the old checkpoints) dropped from [ent origin]

Adding an origin any time after the tier takes over silently swaps the fallback from the remote that holds the checkpoints to a brand-new empty one. Narrower than the original bug — it needs a later origin — but it's the same failure mode, and it's the case persisting the displaced election in the ledger (the option he considered and declined) would have covered.
