- [P2] Cross-repository adoption rejects legitimate Gemini/OpenCode callers — cmd/entire/cli/session_adopt.go:164
   ResolveCallerSession only loads states from the target repository’s session store. During adoption from a different repository, the source state—and its recorded process owner—is therefore unavailable to ancestry resolution. Agents without caller-session environment variables
   (Gemini CLI and OpenCode) cannot be recognized as owning their own source session, so non-interactive adoption is refused unless they incorrectly use --allow-foreign-session. Include the selected source state in caller resolution, or directly compare its owner against current
   process ancestry before falling back to the foreign-session path.
