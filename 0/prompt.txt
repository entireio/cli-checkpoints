The redact package logs pack load failures, rule compile errors, and sample mismatches through the process-default slog logger — bare stderr, which hook contexts swallow — because nothing ever wires it to the CLI's .entire/logs/ sink (there is no slog.SetDefault anywhere in the binary). The happy path logs nothing at all.

Found while investigating a customer security report: they grepped their logs for component=redaction while debugging custom rules, found nothing, and reasonably concluded the rules never ran.
