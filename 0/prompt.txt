### Important

None that block a PR. The two below are the closest:

1. **Spec acceptance criteria 2 and 3 have no automated coverage** (`cmd/entire/cli/integration_test/opencode_subagent_test.go`). "Two concurrent children yield two records with disjoint files" and "a read-only child yields a completed record with no files and a transcript" are covered only by the live probe runs you cite. 2. **`model` on `subagent-stop` is the parent's model, not the child's, and nothing consumes it** (`entire_plugin.ts:175` sends `currentModel ?? Either send `output?.metadata?.model` or drop the field from both sides.

### Minor

3. **`TestInstallHooks_SubagentHooksFireFromParentTaskSignals` pins 15 source literals** (`opencode/hooks_test.go:465-509`). 9. **Plugin comment slightly overclaims restart coverage** (`entire_plugin.ts:28-29`): "the latter two also cover a child whose own `session.created` this process never saw".
