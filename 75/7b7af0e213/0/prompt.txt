Transient first-turn probe failure permanently suppresses the hint (lifecycle.go:421-441). ContextInjectionDecided is set BEFORE the enablement probe -- intentional, to bound to <=1 probe per session. Consequence: a transient timeout/network blip on the first turn marks the session 'decided' and the hint never appears again for that session even though trails are actually enabled. The safe direction (never spams), and documented in the code/state comment, but the failure mode is 'enabled repo, hint silently never shows.' Acceptable; flagging the chosen tradeoff (miss, not retry).
